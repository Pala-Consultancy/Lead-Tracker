Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

The extension is free to install. It works with your PalaTrack account, so the features follow your PalaTrack plan.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome, and other browsers that install extensions from the Chrome Web Store, like Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Do I need to connect LinkedIn?

To publish and schedule posts, connect LinkedIn once on PalaTrack’s LinkedIn Post page (it lasts about 60 days, and PalaTrack reminds you before it ends). Analytics and Leads from posts use the LinkedIn login already in your browser.

Does my team see my posts and numbers?

Your drafts, analytics and DM log are yours. Leads you add go to the boards you choose, where your team can work on them with you.

What happens if a scheduled post can’t go out?

PalaTrack retries for up to 30 minutes. If it still can’t publish, it doesn’t post late: you get a notification and an email with the reason, and you can publish it with one click.
