The problems

You've felt these before

Every one of these came from a real frustration — ours, or a client's.

0 of 9 solved
The problem
Leads live in scattered spreadsheets and DMs, and nobody's sure which version is the current one.
How we solve it
Every lead lives on one shared, real-time board — everyone sees the same, up-to-date pipeline.
The problem
Follow-ups get forgotten and deals go cold because nobody remembered to reach back out.
How we solve it
Automatic reminders nudge you the moment a lead's gone quiet or a call needs a status update.
The problem
You have no real idea what's working — which source, which country, which rep is actually performing.
How we solve it
A full statistics dashboard breaks down win rate, revenue, and performance by source, country, and more.
The problem
Switching between five different tools just to update one lead wastes half your day.
How we solve it
Alexandre, your built-in AI assistant, answers questions about your pipeline without ever leaving the page.
The problem
One accidental delete and a lead's whole history is gone for good.
How we solve it
Every deleted lead lands safely in the Trash, ready to restore anytime — nothing disappears by accident.
The problem
A team spread across countries means half of them are working in a language they didn't choose.
How we solve it
The entire tracker is fully translated into 9 languages, so everyone works comfortably in their own.
The problem
Two people reach out to the same lead because nobody could tell who was already working it.
How we solve it
Every board belongs to one person from the start — so every lead in it is already clearly theirs, with zero setup.
The problem
Finding one specific lead, or a whole segment of them, means scrolling through hundreds of rows by hand.
How we solve it
Filter by source, status, temperature, industry, country, or month — narrow hundreds of leads down to exactly the ones you need in one click.
The problem
Leads from LinkedIn work differently — connection requests, acceptances, and outreach steps that generic CRMs just don't have fields for.
How we solve it
A built-in LinkedIn mode tracks connection requests sent and accepted right alongside the rest of your outreach, with acceptance rate shown automatically in your stats.
