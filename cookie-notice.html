What we actually use

PalaTrack only stores information in your browser that's necessary to make the app work properly and remember your preferences. Nothing here is used for advertising, and nothing is shared with third parties for tracking purposes.

WhatPurposeWhere it's stored
preferredLanguage Remembers which of the 9 supported languages you last selected. Browser local storage
theme Remembers whether you last chose Light or Dark mode. Browser local storage
displayCurrency Remembers your preferred currency format for revenue figures. Browser local storage
Firebase session token Keeps you signed in so you don't have to log in every time you open PalaTrack. Browser local storage, managed by Firebase Authentication
emailForSignIn Temporarily stored only when completing an email invite link, then removed. Browser local storage

Why "local storage" instead of cookies?

Modern web apps like PalaTrack typically use a browser feature called "local storage" rather than traditional cookies for this kind of preference and session data. It works similarly — small pieces of information saved in your browser — but isn't automatically sent along with every request the way cookies are, and exists only on your own device.

Managing this yourself

You can clear all of this at any time through your browser's own settings (usually under "Clear browsing data" or "Site settings"). Doing so will sign you out and reset your language, theme, and currency preferences back to their defaults the next time you visit.

Questions

If you have questions about this notice, use the Send Feedback option in Settings, or reach out to your PalaTrack administrator directly.
