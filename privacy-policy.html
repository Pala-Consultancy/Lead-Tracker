What this policy covers

This policy explains what information PalaTrack collects when you or your team use it, how that information is stored, and who can see it. PalaTrack is an internal tool built for Pala Consultancy and its clients — it is not a public product, and access is limited to people who have been invited by an administrator.

Information we collect

When you create an account or are invited to PalaTrack, we store:

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, company, role, and a profile photo if you choose to add one.
  • Lead and client data: the names, companies, contact details, and deal information that you or your teammates enter into the tracker.
  • Usage preferences: your chosen language and light/dark mode setting.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate PalaTrack for your team: displaying your profile to teammates, tracking leads and revenue, generating statistics, and letting administrators manage who has access. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where your data is stored

PalaTrack is built on Google Firebase, a cloud infrastructure platform. Your account information and lead data are stored in Firebase's Firestore database and protected by security rules that only allow signed-in team members to access data belonging to your organization's boards.

Who can see your data

Only people who have been explicitly invited to your PalaTrack workspace can sign in and view boards, leads, and team information. Within the workspace, all authenticated team members can see shared boards and leads by design, since PalaTrack is built as a shared team tool. Administrators additionally have the ability to manage team member roles.

Feedback you send us

If you submit feedback through the Feedback form, we store your message along with your name and email (if you're signed in) so we can follow up or ask clarifying questions. Feedback is used solely to improve PalaTrack and is not shared outside Pala Consultancy.

Data retention

Your information is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you leave the organization, an administrator can remove your account, at which point your personal profile information is deleted. Historical lead and deal records tied to shared boards may be retained as part of the team's business records.

Your choices

You can update your name, phone number, company, role, language, and profile photo at any time from Manage account. If you'd like your account fully removed, contact your administrator or use the Feedback form to reach us directly.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please use the Send Feedback option in Settings, or reach out to your Pala Consultancy administrator directly.
