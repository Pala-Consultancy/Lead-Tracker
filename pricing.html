Questions

Frequently asked

Still deciding? Here's what people usually ask before switching.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle, and your boards and leads stay exactly as they are either way.

Is there a free trial?

Reach out through the contact form and we'll set you up with a trial board, so you can try PalaTrack with your own leads before committing to a plan.

What currencies do you support?

Plans are priced in USD, but once you're in, you can display your pipeline's revenue in any of the currencies available in Settings.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your boards and leads stay intact for 30 days after cancellation in case you change your mind. After that, they're permanently removed.
